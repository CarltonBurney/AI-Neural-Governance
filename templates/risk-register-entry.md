## Rules

**Write the risk statement as cause → event → consequence.** "Bias" is not a risk statement. *"Training on historical hiring decisions (cause) causes systematically lower rankings for candidates with employment gaps (event), reducing interview access for applicants who took parental leave (consequence)"* is one — it names what to test and what to fix.

**Residual risk is accepted by a named individual**, never by a committee or a function. If no one will put their name to it, it has not been accepted.

**Control effectiveness of "Not tested" is not a rating** — it is an open action. A control that has never been tested cannot be relied on to reduce a rating.

**Expired exceptions and lapsed recertifications enter the register automatically** and trigger suspension of the associated system, not a reminder.

## Standing entries every T1 system carries

| | Risk |
|---|---|
| 1 | Oversight degradation — human review becomes nominal over time as trust accumulates faster than vigilance |
| 2 | Vendor model change alters behavior without notice |
| 3 | Scope creep — the system is used outside its approved envelope |
| 4 | Reviewer pool falls below minimum through attrition or volume spike |
| 5 | Concentration — no substitution path for a critical third-party dependency |
| 6 | Drift — input distribution moves away from the evaluation set |
