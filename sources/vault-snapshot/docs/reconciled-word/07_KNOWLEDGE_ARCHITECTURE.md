07 KNOWLEDGE ARCHITECTURE
AI-Neural-Governance documentation set
The Knowledge Problem
Organizations usually already have the information they need. What they do not have is a reliable way to connect the right person to the right information at the right point in execution.
Microsoft-Native Grounding Layer
SharePoint for policy, SOP, and document storage
Teams for conversation and operational updates
OneDrive for working files
Outlook for email and action context
Calendars, meetings, and documents as work context
Role of the Assistant
The assistant does not replace the knowledge base. It reduces the friction required to use it. It turns storage into access, and access into guided execution.
