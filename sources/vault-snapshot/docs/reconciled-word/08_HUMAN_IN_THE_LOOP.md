08 HUMAN IN THE LOOP
AI-Neural-Governance documentation set
Core Principle
AI assists. Humans decide. Accountability remains with people.
Why This Matters
The fastest way to lose trust in an AI-enabled system is to let outputs blur the line between assistance and authority. Human review is how organizations preserve judgment, compliance, and accountability while still benefiting from speed.
Places Where Human Review Matters Most
Sensitive data handling
External communication
Policy interpretation
Approvals and exceptions
Workflow actions that change records or access
